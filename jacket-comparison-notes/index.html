A matched-measurement method for jacket shopping

Write down the measurement endpoints before comparing values. A sleeve taken from the shoulder seam cannot be compared directly with a center-back measurement. When the endpoints match, record the two readings and calculate the difference. Try the reference jacket with your intended layers and decide which differences would matter in motion. The Fashionreps jacket catalogue offers candidates and available QC photos. Keep any value with an unclear method out of the comparison until you can verify it.
